Downwind

Fly / Log / Share

The social logbook for private pilots. Your phone records the flight, Downwind turns it into a logbook entry and a flight you can share with your wingmen.

Coming to the App Store in January 2027Join the beta

Auto-record

Downwind notices the take-off and the landing near an airfield and records block times and the track – even with the phone in your pocket.

Swiss national map

Every flight on the swisstopo map, in 2D or 3D, with a replay and a short video to share.

Logbook

Times, landings, night, PIC – as CSV and as a printable PDF. Plus the 90-day rule and your expiry dates at a glance.

Wingmen & club

Share flights only with the pilots you confirm. Club page, fly-outs, chat and a little friendly collecting – never racing.
