Auto-record

Just fly.

Downwind notices take-off and landing near an airfield and records block times and the track – even with the phone in your pocket. After landing you review and share.

Swiss national map

Every flight on the map.

Your tracks on the swisstopo map, in 2D or 3D, with a replay, a short video to share – and GAFOR routes and classics to collect.

Logbook

Current and valid at a glance.

Times, landings, night and PIC as CSV and printable PDF. The 90-day rule, your expiry dates and reminders before they run out.

Wingmen & club

Share with the people you fly with.

Flights are visible only to wingmen you confirm. Club page, fly-outs and fly-ins, chat – and friendly collecting, never racing.

A look inside the app

Downwind is not for navigation. Plan and fly with official charts, AIP, VAC and NOTAM.
