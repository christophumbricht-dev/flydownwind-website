2. What Downwind is

Downwind is a social flight log for private pilots: it records and stores your flights, shows them on maps, lets you share them with wingmen, family and clubs, and offers challenges, a theory refresher and other community features.

Some features are optional or only available in some countries (e.g. the Swiss national map). We may change, extend or remove features; we tell you in the app about important changes.

3. Not for navigation – not an official logbook

Downwind is not for navigation and not for flight planning. Maps, traffic circuits, routes, weather information and all numbers are for information only. Plan and fly with official charts, AIP, VAC, NOTAM and an official briefing. The pilot in command alone decides about every flight.

Downwind does not replace your official pilot logbook, the records of your flight school or any document required by law. Numbers such as "take-offs and landings in the last 90 days" are counts only, not a statement that you may carry passengers or that a rating is valid.

Operate the app before taxi and after shutdown only – never in critical phases of flight. Never fly lower, faster, longer or in worse weather because of Downwind, its challenges or routes.

4. Your account

You must be at least 16 years old to create an account. Give correct information about yourself; one account per person; keep your sign-in safe.

Passengers and family members who only follow a pilot also need to be at least 16 years old, or use Downwind with the consent of a parent.

5. Your content and the rules for it

You keep the rights to your flights, photos, comments and other content. You allow us to store, show and process it as far as needed to run Downwind for you and for the people you share it with (according to your visibility settings).

Only post content you have the right to post, and only show other people (e.g. passengers) with their consent.

Zero tolerance for objectionable content. Not allowed: insults, harassment, hate, threats, sexual or violent content, content that endangers others, spam, illegal content, false information that endangers flight safety, and calls to fly in an unsafe or illegal way. We remove such content and may suspend or delete the account without notice.

6. Reporting and blocking

You can report flights, comments, reviews, messages, profiles and quiz questions in the app and block other pilots. We check reports as soon as possible, usually within 24 hours, and remove content that breaks these terms. Blocked pilots no longer see your content and cannot contact you.

7. Privacy
